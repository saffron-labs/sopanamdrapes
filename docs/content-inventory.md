## 11. Things to fix in the rebuild (content, not design)

These came out of the scrape and will hurt a premium version:

- **Prototype language is public.** Terms end with a "Prototype note" saying payments auto-approve and OTPs are shown in the app, and point to a README. The login page says "two-factor, like the proposal specifies." Both read as a student demo.
- **Wedding has zero products.** The two most bridal sarees (Banarasi, Kanjivaram) have no occasion set, so the Wedding filter and the "Wedding Edit" tile lead nowhere.
- **"Bridal whites & diamonds"** is promised on the home page but there are no white sarees or diamond pieces in the catalogue.
- **"7 pieces per curated look"** — no default look actually has 7 pieces (max is 4; the cotton saree has none). Either fill the defaults or reword.
- **Placeholder phone number** (+91 90000 12345).
- **One photo per product**, no colour variants, no reviews — "What people are saying" renders empty.
- **"100% authentic"** sits next to gold-tone/gold-plated/oxidised jewellery; the copy should say what the pieces are (fashion/imitation jewellery) to stay honest.
- **Missing pages** a premium store needs: About/story, Contact, FAQ, Shipping, Privacy policy, Size/blouse guide, Care instructions.
- **Missing product data** a premium store needs: saree length/width, blouse piece length, weight, weave origin/region, care (dry clean etc.), dispatch time, ornament material and dimensions.
- Policy says COD has a limit but never states the amount; no shipping charges or delivery timelines anywhere.
